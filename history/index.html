Company story

Ask for Task is being built as an operating house for useful apps.

ASK FOR TASK LTD was founded by Nikita Piazenko to build and establish digital services that help communities communicate, coordinate, complete tasks, and solve everyday problems with more trust.

01
Origin

A self-made company direction

Ask for Task begins with a simple belief: useful software should do real work for real people. The company is built around finding practical problems and turning them into dependable services.

02
Reason

Why the company exists

Many community problems are too specific for generic platforms and too operational for simple websites. Ask for Task is designed to define the problem, build the product, support it, and keep improving it.

03
First venture

Pinglo proves the method

Pinglo is the first public example: a lost-and-found service focused on clearer reporting, safer claims, accountable returns, and less confusion when people need help.

04
Build scope

Systems beyond one app

Ask for Task can shape, design, build, and operate community-focused apps, service platforms, task coordination tools, support systems, and digital products that need a clear route from idea to reliable use.

05
Next

Practical progress through useful networks

Ask for Task will continue developing community apps where practical software can reduce confusion, create employment-minded systems, and make local coordination more dependable.
