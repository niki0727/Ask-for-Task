Company story

Ask for Task builds tools for small real-world problems.

ASK FOR TASK LTD is developing practical digital products for everyday coordination, beginning with Pinglo.

Why Pinglo

Lost-and-found coordination often happens across messy chats, public posts, and unclear claims. Pinglo focuses that process into reporting, matching, verification, and safer return flows.

What Comes Next

Ask for Task will continue building products that make ordinary coordination feel calmer, clearer, and more accountable.
