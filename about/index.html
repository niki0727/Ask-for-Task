About

Nikita Piazenko founded Ask for Task to build useful services from practical problems.

ASK FOR TASK LTD is a product and operating company. It exists to shape community problems into apps, systems, and services that people can rely on.

Founder

Nikita Piazenko

Nikita Piazenko is the founder of ASK FOR TASK LTD. The company reflects a self-made approach to product building: find a real task, understand the people around it, build the service properly, and keep improving it until it becomes dependable.

Company

Ask for Task

Ask for Task works on apps and operational systems for coordination, support, local services, community tools, requests, claims, moderation, and practical workflows that need trust built in from the beginning.

How we think

Useful software should feel accountable after launch.

Ask for Task is interested in products that do real jobs for real people. The work is not only designing screens. It is understanding the task, building the system around it, supporting the users, and improving the service over time.
