Your information in Studio

Studio is operated by ASK FOR TASK LTD, trading as A4T Studio. This notice supplements the A4T Studio privacy policy for creator accounts, private projects and creator enquiries.

Your account and profile

We use your email to verify access and return you to your saved work. Your profile stays private until you choose to show it in Explore. A published profile includes the name, photo, bio, specialties, availability and links you choose to share. Your sign-in email stays private.

You can edit or unpublish your profile in Studio. Deleting your account removes your account identity, creator profile, photo, projects and tasks. Enquiry correspondence may be retained separately under the main policy.

Projects and enquiries

Your saved projects and tasks are available through your account. Sending a creator enquiry gives A4T your name, email, chosen creator and brief so we can respond and coordinate the next step. A4T operators can review these enquiries and keep private delivery notes.

How Studio runs

Supabase provides sign-in and the PostgreSQL database in London. Render hosts the application in Frankfurt. Resend sends sign-in emails and A4T enquiry alerts. These providers process information to operate Studio and may process service information internationally.

Sign-in uses a first-party session cookie. Short-lived submission counters use hashed keys. Reading a public source link requests that page from Studio's server. Images linked in a portfolio load from the websites where they are hosted.

AI drafting is currently disabled. If enabled later, sending source or project text to OpenAI will require your explicit choice in the form.

Backups and your choices

A4T makes encrypted database backups on Nikita's Mac. Verified snapshots are retained for up to 30 days, so deleted records can remain in those snapshots until they expire.

For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, email admin@askfortask.co.uk. The main privacy policy describes your rights and how to contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
