A practical route

Fund one clear stage at a time.

The first proposal recommends the smallest useful stage. Each stage has its own deliverables, schedule, responsibilities, and price before work begins.

01 / DefineScoped stage

Product definition

Clarify the problem, users, workflows, constraints, and the smallest useful release before committing to a build.

  • Product objective and audience
  • Core journeys and requirements
  • Recommended first release
02 / PrototypeScoped stage

Prototype and interface

Make the main journeys visible and testable before engineering decisions become expensive.

  • Information and screen structure
  • Interactive prototype
  • Design direction and test notes
03 / BuildProject quote

Build and launch

We choose and manage the people needed to build, test, prepare, and release the agreed product.

  • Product and interface design
  • Web, mobile, backend, or integration development
  • Testing and release preparation
  • Launch, handover, and early improvement
04 / ContinueSeparate scope

Support and releases

Plan fixes, platform updates, user feedback, and new releases after the initial handover.

  • Support responsibilities
  • Release priorities and review cycles
  • Quoted maintenance or improvement stages

Price depends on the stage, type of app, features, platforms, integrations, team, testing, and deadline. The written scope and quote come before paid work. Any material change to time or cost must be approved first.

Common questions

What clients ask first.

Do you only build community apps?

No. We work on consumer products, company workflows, customer services, internal tools, operational systems, and other focused app ideas.

Can I come with an early idea?

Yes. A rough idea, repeated task, or problem that should be easier is enough for the first conversation.

How is the work priced?

The quote follows the product stage, platforms, workflows, integrations, professional roles, testing, launch work, and support required. Scope, schedule, and price are agreed before paid work begins.

How long does development take?

Time depends on the product stage, platforms, features, integrations, testing, review speed, and launch requirements. The proposed schedule is included with the written scope.

What will be handed over?

The scope lists the app build, source code, design files, documentation, accounts, store access, and support included. Handover happens after the agreed delivery and relevant invoices are complete.

Can you support the app after launch?

Yes. Support can cover fixes, platform updates, user feedback, new features, release preparation, and ongoing product planning through a separate support stage or partnership.
