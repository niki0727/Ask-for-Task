Common questions

What clients usually need to know.

Can you work with our current website?

Yes. We can first review the current content, analytics, hosting, platform, and technical condition, then define what should be kept, improved, rebuilt, or moved.

How much will the website cost?

The quote depends on pages, content, design, functions, migration, integrations, testing, and timing. We agree the deliverables and price in writing before paid work begins.

Does the build include SEO?

The scope can include technical and on-page SEO essentials, analytics, and search-ready page structure. Ongoing content, authority building, and search improvement are separate work.

What do we own after launch?

The scope states which accounts, licences, source files, content, and access are handed over. The agreed work is transferred after the relevant invoices are paid.
