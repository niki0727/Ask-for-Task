Photography bookings

Every shoot is different. Before a date is booked, the written quote will set out what we are photographing, where and when the shoot will happen, the price, expected delivery and how the images will be used.

Time and delivery

Duration depends on the brief, location, access, number of subjects, and intended use. Food and hospitality shoots often take two to four hours. Evening events usually take four to six hours, although longer and full-day coverage can be arranged.

A typical four-hour event is normally edited and delivered within seven calendar days, with 14 days as the maximum. Longer or more complex projects will have their own delivery date in the quote.

Payment

Some shoots need a booking payment to hold the date. Where requested, this is usually 30% of the total and comes off the final balance. The quote will make all payment dates clear.

If plans change

Please tell us as soon as you can. We will do our best to move the shoot to another available date. If we cannot agree a suitable new date, we will return the booking payment. The same approach applies if weather, illness, access or flight safety prevents the work from going ahead.

Using the photographs

The quote will explain where and how the delivered images can be used. Unless we agree otherwise, A4T Studio keeps the copyright. If you need exclusive use, copyright ownership or another type of licence, tell us before the quote is agreed.

A4T Studio will not publish private commissioned work without permission.
