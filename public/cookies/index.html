Website measurement

Cookies and Analytics

A short account of what this website measures and, just as importantly, what it does not.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Current cookie use

Ask for Task’s website code does not currently set cookies. It does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, fingerprinting, session replay, or cross-site tracking. The site therefore does not show a non-essential cookie banner.

Your browser or network provider may have its own settings and storage that are outside Ask for Task’s control. Cloudflare also processes technical requests to deliver and protect the website, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Aggregate website information

Cloudflare may provide operational information needed to serve, secure, and diagnose the website. Ask for Task uses this at a service level rather than creating advertising audiences or cross-site profiles.

Outbound partner-link requests

Links to approved partner, project, app-store, and portfolio destinations pass through a first-party Ask for Task redirect. The server records only four things: the approved partner slug, a controlled source-page label, the UTC date, and an aggregate request count.

The counter does not create a visitor ID and does not store a full IP address, name, email address, fingerprint, or browsing profile. It is not connected to enquiries, reviews, or professional applications. Bots and link scanners can also request these links, so the totals describe outbound partner-link requests rather than guaranteed human visits.

Why the counts are used

The totals help us understand which public work examples and partner destinations are useful, decide what evidence should be easier to find, and maintain links that people actually request. Daily aggregate counts are kept for up to 24 months.

Your choices

Because the counter does not identify a visitor, there is no individual profile to switch off with an opt-out cookie. You can avoid a measured redirect by not opening the outbound link, or by visiting a destination you already know directly. For a question or objection about this measurement, email admin@askfortask.co.uk.

Future changes

This page will be updated before Ask for Task introduces materially different analytics, non-essential cookies, or tracking. Any required consent choice will be added before that technology is enabled.
