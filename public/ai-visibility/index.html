After the preview, the paid pilot covers one service in one market. We agree the buyer questions, two AI search tools and up to three website pages before work begins.

Establish a baseline

We agree eight buyer questions and run each twice on two search tools. That gives up to 32 observations in each round. We record whether you were named, which sources appeared and whether they support key claims.

Make the agreed fixes

We implement and verify up to three fixes. Examples include clarifying a service, linking to a relevant project or fixing an issue that keeps search engines from reading an agreed page. Your scope names who owns each change.

Retest the same questions

About four weeks after the changes go live, we repeat the questions under comparable conditions. You see before-and-after counts for each tool and any checks we could not compare.

Keep the evidence

You receive a downloadable log with the questions, observed answers and sources, plus a record of the pages we changed, how we checked them and what still needs attention.

Before you start

Who is the pilot for?

Specialist service firms with an existing website, a defined offer and someone who can approve factual and website updates. We start with English-language websites serving the UK and can assess other markets where the language, tool access and scope fit.

What does the free preview include?

Three buyer questions on one agreed search tool, a dated record of the answers and sources, and one practical recommendation. It is a small sample, with no website changes or ongoing subscription. The paid pilot is scoped separately.

Can we work together outside the UK?

Yes. Delivery is remote. We agree the market and language, record where each test was run and keep the conditions comparable at retest. A UK-focused question run from Lithuania is labelled that way; it is not presented as a test of every UK buyer’s experience.

Which AI search tools will you check?

We agree two tools before work starts, based on your market and which search features we can test reliably there. Options include ChatGPT Search and Bing’s AI search.

What do you need from us?

Start with your website, one service and the market you want to reach. Before we change any pages, we agree who will verify business facts and who can approve and publish the edits.

How much does the pilot cost?

Once we know your website, target market and who can make changes, we can propose a written scope with deliverables, timing and cost for your approval.

Can you guarantee that AI will recommend us?

AI answers vary by tool, location and time. We can verify the website changes we deliver and show the answers we observed. Each platform decides what it shows and cites.
