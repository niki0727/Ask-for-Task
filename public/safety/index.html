Safety Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Operated by ASK FOR TASK LTD.

Product Safety

  • Pinglo is designed to reduce unnecessary public sharing during lost-and-found reports.
  • Claim and return flows should use verification before sensitive information is exchanged.
  • Users should avoid sharing passports, banking details, full addresses, or highly sensitive documents.

Abuse Handling

  • Use product reporting tools for scams, harassment, suspicious claims, or unsafe behaviour.
  • Use blocking controls where available to stop unwanted interaction.
  • Safety reports may be reviewed for moderation, fraud prevention, and dispute handling.

Contact
