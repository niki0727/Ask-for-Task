Background

From Lithuania and Italy to creative work in London.

Nikita grew up in Lithuania, then moved to Italy and later Denmark before settling in London. Each move brought new languages, cultures and people into his life and creative work.

Read more about Nikita's backgroundRead less

Photography became a profession as well as a way of seeing. Work across portraits, events, hospitality, brands and aerial photography taught him how to understand a brief, prepare for changing conditions, work carefully with people, and deliver images with a clear use. Many clients return for further assignments, including My Soho Times, where he works as the in-house photographer.

A4T Studio grew from the same approach: listen first, make the goal clear, invite the right people into the work, and stay close to the project until it is finished.

Nikita is the founder and project lead. His role is to understand the brief, organise the work and coordinate delivery. Professional design, development and specialist roles are agreed within each project scope.
