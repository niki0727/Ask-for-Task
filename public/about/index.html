Meet Nikita Piazenko, founder of Ask for Task.

Nikita founded Ask for Task after five years of professional photography. Now based in London, he brings the same curiosity, preparation, and care to creative work, digital products, and wider business projects.

Background

A point of view shaped by places, people, and practice.

Nikita grew up in Lithuania and began taking photographs at 11 after moving to Italy. Before settling in London, he lived in four countries and learned to adapt to different languages, cultures, places, and ways of working.

Read more about Nikita's backgroundRead less

Photography became a profession as well as a way of seeing. Five years of client work taught him how to understand a brief, prepare for changing conditions, work carefully with people, and deliver something that has a clear use.

Ask for Task grew from the same approach: listen first, make the goal clear, invite the right people into the work, and stay close to the project until it is finished.

Nikita remains the first point of contact and project lead. He does not present himself as the expert in every discipline; the company works with selected professionals when a brief needs skills beyond his own.

Work in practice

Different work, guided by the same care.

Photography and product work both begin with attention, a clear purpose, and respect for the people who will use the result.
