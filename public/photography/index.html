Booking

Simple, flexible booking.

Every shoot is different. We agree the time, price, delivery and intended image use in writing before the date is booked.

01 / Time

Duration follows the brief.

Hospitality shoots often take two to four hours. Evening events usually take four to six hours, but longer and full-day coverage can be arranged.

02 / Delivery

Most event edits arrive within a week.

A typical four-hour event is normally delivered within seven calendar days and no later than 14 days. Longer or multi-day projects receive their own written delivery date.

03 / Payment

Some bookings need 30% to hold the date.

Any booking payment comes off the final balance. The quote will make the amount and payment dates clear before you decide.

04 / Plans change

Tell us as soon as you can.

We will do our best to move the shoot to another available date. If we cannot agree a suitable new date, we will return the booking payment.
