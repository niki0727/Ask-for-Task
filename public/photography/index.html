The photographer

Five years behind the camera.

Nikita Piazenko is a self-taught photographer from Lithuania. He began at 11 after moving to Italy and lived in four countries before settling in London. That experience shaped an interest in people, cities, architecture, and the details that give a place its character.

Read more about Nikita's experienceRead less

Across five years of professional work, he has photographed portraits, events, food, venues, and commercial content in daylight and at night. He learned manual photography on a Nikon D5000 and now works with a Nikon Z6 II.

Lightroom and Photoshop support the photography workflow, with Premiere Pro, Illustrator, and After Effects available when a project also needs video, motion, or design.
