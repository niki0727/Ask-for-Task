Company story

Ask for Task is built as an operating house for useful apps.

ASK FOR TASK LTD was founded by Nikita Piazenko to build and establish digital services that help communities coordinate, complete tasks, and handle everyday problems with more trust.

Why the Company Exists

Many useful community problems are too specific for generic platforms and too operational for simple websites. Ask for Task is designed to do the complete work: define the problem, build the product, support it, and keep improving it.

First Venture

Pinglo is the first public example of this method. It focuses on lost-and-found coordination through clearer reporting, safer claims, and accountable return flows.

What We Can Build

Ask for Task can help shape, design, build, and operate community-focused apps, practical service platforms, task coordination tools, support systems, and digital products that need a clear route from idea to reliable use.

What Comes Next

Ask for Task will continue developing community apps where practical software can reduce confusion, create employment-minded systems, and make local coordination more dependable.
