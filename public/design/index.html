Common questions

What to agree before design begins.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the item list, intended use, sizes or formats, deadline, budget, and any existing logo, copy, photography, or brand guide. We can help define what is missing.

How many concepts and revisions are included?

The written scope states the number of concepts, review points, and revision rounds. New directions or extra formats are quoted before that work begins.

Which files will I receive?

The handover list may include print-ready PDFs, web images, logo formats, editable source files, templates, or guidelines where these are part of the agreed scope.

Can you arrange photography, copy, or printing?

Yes. Photography, copywriting, production preparation, and print coordination can be added when they are needed for the final result.
