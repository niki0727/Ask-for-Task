Mandate

Not a side brand. An operating company.

Ask for Task turns practical problems into dependable digital services. The company is self-made, employer-minded, and built around completing real tasks: from identifying the problem to launching, supporting, improving, and establishing the app in public use.

Core services

What Ask for Task can do for others.

We work on practical app ideas where the goal is not only a nice interface, but a service people can actually use, trust, and return to.

01

App concept and validation

Shape a community problem into a product plan: user flows, core features, launch scope, risk points, and what should be built first.

02

Product design and build

Create clean, practical web and app experiences with the structure needed for accounts, reports, matching, messages, support, or administration.

03

Community service systems

Build tools for lost and found, local help, task coordination, listings, claims, requests, moderation, or operational workflows.

04

Launch and operation

Help establish the product after launch: feedback loops, support handling, safety standards, privacy wording, and improvement cycles.

Operating model

We build with responsibility from the first sketch.

01

Find community pressure

We look for repeated everyday friction: lost items, local coordination, trust gaps, handoffs, support requests, and admin that should be simpler.

02

Design the service

Each product gets clear flows, privacy expectations, support routes, moderation thinking, and a practical path from first use to long-term operation.

03

Establish the app

Ask for Task does not only launch. It maintains, learns, improves, and keeps the service accountable as the community around it grows.

Founder

Nikita Piazenko founded Ask for Task to build useful companies from practical problems.

Nikita Piazenko is the founder of ASK FOR TASK LTD. The company reflects a self-made approach to product building: find a real task, understand the people around it, build the service properly, and keep improving it until it becomes dependable.

Ask for Task is open to conversations with people, organisations, and communities that need apps or digital systems for coordination, support, trust, or everyday operations.

Current venture

Pinglo is one example of the Ask for Task method.
