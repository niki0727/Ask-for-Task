Illustrative project examples

These are example briefs, not completed client projects. They show how we would shape the work, select the skills, and divide delivery into practical stages.

01
Hotel & hospitality

A smoother guest journey and more direct bookings.

A hotel wants to rely less on third-party booking sites, reward returning guests, and make the stay easier from arrival to check-out.

View route
Example objective

Connect booking, loyalty, service, and communication.

The first stage would confirm the hotel systems already in use, where guests lose time, what staff can support, and which features would create a useful first release.

App
Guest registration, booking details, loyalty points, offers, check-in information, room or service requests, dining and spa reservations, and a digital key where the hotel’s systems support it.
Website
Rooms, availability, direct-booking integration, dining, events, local guides, accessibility information, and clear reasons to book directly.
Visual identity
A consistent system for the website, app, signs, room information, menus, offers, email, and social content.
Copywriting
Room descriptions, guest policies, booking messages, pre-arrival email, in-app prompts, local guides, offers, and follow-up communication.
Social media
Content around rooms, food, staff, the local area, events, seasonal offers, and reasons to return.
Launch support
Booking and property-system checks, staff workflows, guest testing, analytics, launch content, and a plan for the first month of feedback.
02
Café & food

A recognisable café with easier ordering and stronger repeat visits.

A growing café wants a clearer identity, more returning customers, and a simple way to manage orders, rewards, menus, and local promotion.

View route
Example objective

Turn everyday visits into a simple customer system.

The work could begin with the brand and menu, then add ordering and loyalty once the team, payment setup, and daily service flow are ready.

App
Customer account, points and rewards, order ahead, click and collect, favourites, payment integration, personalised offers, and order updates.
Website
Live menu, allergens, locations, opening times, table enquiries, catering, events, gift cards, and links into ordering.
Visual identity
Logo, colour and type, menus, cups, packaging, signage, uniforms, loyalty graphics, and reusable social templates.
Copywriting
Brand story, menu language, product descriptions, catering pages, loyalty messages, email, and seasonal campaigns.
Social media
New products, people behind the café, preparation, customer moments, local partnerships, and seasonal content.
Launch support
Staff onboarding, test orders, QR and counter materials, local launch campaign, customer feedback, and early performance checks.
03
Fitness & membership

A membership brand that is easier to join and use.

A fitness studio wants to reduce booking administration, fill more classes, and give members one reliable place for schedules, payments, and updates.

View route
Example objective

Make joining, booking, and returning feel straightforward.

The project would map the member and staff journey first, then decide whether the best first release is a website, member portal, mobile app, or a staged combination.

App
Membership account, class booking, waitlists, reminders, passes, payments, trainer messages, and simple member updates.
Website
Class schedule, trainers, locations, trial booking, memberships, pricing, FAQs, contact, and useful search pages.
Visual identity
A flexible look for the space, website, app, class timetable, staff materials, campaigns, and merchandise.
Copywriting
Membership options, class descriptions, trainer profiles, onboarding, reminders, policies, and campaign copy.
Social media
Trainer expertise, class formats, member stories with permission, practical advice, timetables, and trial offers.
Launch support
Membership migration, booking tests, staff guidance, analytics, launch communication, and support through the first booking cycles.

Writing within the brand

Brand copy and ghostwriting are different jobs.

Brand copywriting covers the words used to explain and promote the business: websites, products, campaigns, email, and social media.

Specialist writing and ghostwriting covers deeper editorial work written in a person’s voice. Our dedicated specialist works mainly in sport. Other subjects can be considered when the brief, access, and research are a good fit.
