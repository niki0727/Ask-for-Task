Starting point

Deep expertise needed a clear public route.

DMAR began with deep marine operations experience and the plan for an independent consultancy. It needed a public offer that clients could understand quickly.

Read more about the starting pointRead less

Potential clients may arrive with very different needs: offshore wind, subsea cable work, towage, heavy transport, marine civils, project cargo, or assurance support. The identity and website had to connect that range without making the business feel vague.

The website therefore had to do more than look established. It needed to explain the offer, help a visitor recognise the right service, support project enquiries, and leave room for evidence, recruitment, and future growth.

What Ask for Task delivered

Four connected parts, built from scratch.

The identity, offer, website, and operating routes were developed as one system rather than separate pieces.

  1. 01

    Logo and visual identity

    A recognisable DMAR mark, professional colour system, typography, and visual direction suited to technical marine work.

    Logo / colour / type / image direction
  2. 02

    Commercial structure
