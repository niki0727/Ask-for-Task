Pinglo: one lost-and-found product across iPhone and web.

Pinglo brings reports, sightings, claim checks, messages, safer returns, and private community feeds into one synced service. A4T Studio shaped the product, identity, delivery, launch, and continued operation.

View on the App Store Open Pinglo on web
The problem

A familiar situation with no consistent process.

When an item is lost or found, information is spread across venues, social posts, messages, and informal handovers. People may not know where to report it, how to identify a genuine match, or how to arrange a safe return.

Pinglo was created to organise those steps across a phone or browser, while giving campuses, venues, and workplaces their own private feed.

The result

A live service for individuals and busy places.

Pinglo is live on iPhone and in the browser. People can browse the feed, post reports, send sightings, verify claims, message privately, and join place-based communities with the same synced account.

Hotels, universities, stations, venues, gyms, and managed buildings can also use Pinglo as one front door for lost property, hand-in guidance, safety alerts, and safer returns.
