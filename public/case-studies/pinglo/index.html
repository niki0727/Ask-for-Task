Pinglo: a clearer way to get lost things back.

When students lose something, it’s hard to know where to look or who to ask. We built Pinglo to make reporting, checking a match and arranging a return easier, from an iPhone or a browser.

View on the App Store Open Pinglo on web
The problem

Lost property has too many places to look.

On a campus, a phone left behind might turn up at reception, in a group chat or at the library. A report gives people one place to start. When there’s a possible match, the owner can get a notification.

A private detail helps check ownership, then the two people can arrange a public handover in chat. Universities can also run a private feed with their own hand-in points.
