Pinglo: from an everyday problem to a live iPhone app.

Pinglo gives lost-and-found reports, claims, messages, and returns one clearer route. A4T Studio shaped the product, identity, delivery, launch, public website, and continued development.

View on the App Store Visit Pinglo
The problem

A familiar situation with no consistent process.

When an item is lost or found, information is spread across venues, social posts, messages, and informal handovers. People may not know where to report it, how to identify a genuine match, or how to arrange a safe return.

Pinglo was created to make those steps easier to understand and manage from a phone.
