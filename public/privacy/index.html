Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

ASK FOR TASK LTD operates Ask for Task and Pinglo. We process personal data only where needed to provide support, answer enquiries, operate our products, and meet legal or safety obligations.

Data we process

  • Contact details you submit, such as name, email address, topic, project region, budget range, target date, and message. Answers sent through the guided project assistant are combined into the same kind of contact enquiry.
  • Review details you submit, such as your name, email address, company or project, service, relationship to the work, review text, and publication preference.
  • Professional application details, such as your CV, location, skills, availability, portfolio links, and the kinds of projects you would like to join.
  • Product support information related to Pinglo or future Ask for Task services.
  • Technical logs needed to keep the website and product services secure and reliable.

How we use data

We use submitted details to respond to enquiries, assess and match professionals to current or future projects, review client and collaborator feedback, provide product support, handle privacy or safety requests, and improve service reliability.

Reviews and publication

A submitted review is private by default. Selecting the optional publication permission allows us to consider using it, but does not publish it automatically. Before publishing a named review, we may verify the project and contact you to confirm the final wording and attribution.

Review records are normally kept for up to 24 months after submission or the most recent contact. We may keep an approved published review and its permission record while it remains in use, unless you withdraw permission or ask us to remove it.

Professional profiles and CVs

Professional profiles are not published as a public directory. A submitted CV is delivered privately to ASK FOR TASK LTD through our email provider, while the structured application details are recorded so we can search for suitable professionals when projects arise.

We normally keep professional application details for up to 12 months after the most recent contact, unless a working relationship begins, you ask us to delete them sooner, or a longer period is required for legal or dispute handling.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare to operate the website and database, and Resend to deliver website emails and attached CVs. These providers process data only to supply those services. We do not sell professional profiles or CVs.

Your controls

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. Some operational records may be retained where required for security, dispute handling, or legal compliance.
