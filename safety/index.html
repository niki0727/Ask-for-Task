Company standards

Standards for useful systems people can trust.

Last updated: May 3, 2026. Operated by ASK FOR TASK LTD.

Responsible Product Building

  • Products should have a clear purpose, useful service route, and practical value for the people using them.
  • Communication flows should reduce confusion, protect private details, and make support routes visible.
  • Trust points such as claims, requests, handoffs, and identity-sensitive moments should be designed carefully from the start.

Community Safety

  • Ask for Task builds with privacy, moderation, misuse prevention, and user support in mind.
  • Products should avoid unnecessary sharing of sensitive documents, full addresses, banking details, or private information.
  • Reports about scams, harassment, suspicious behaviour, or unsafe use may be reviewed for safety and dispute handling.

Operational Reliability

  • Useful apps need maintenance after launch, not only design and release.
  • Feedback, bug reports, support requests, and improvement cycles are part of the Ask for Task operating model.
  • When a product grows, the service system around it should grow with it.
